# Iteration, edit and delivery

## Review every take like a director
Watch it, then write a concrete fix list. Keep what works (acting, camera energy) and change only what is broken.

| Problem seen | How to fix it in the prompt |
|---|---|
| The hook moment looks fake or silly (a fall) | Spell out the physics step by step: the heel catches the paper edge, the ankle rolls, the body follows its weight sideways, the hip and palms take the floor |
| A set feels empty or wrong (an empty runway) | Add background people from the group reference, and state where they wait and that they stay |
| A character appears in the wrong place (the assistant's photo among the models) | Add a role lock: "this woman is never on the runway, never in the line, never among the models" |
| The wrong accent | Describe the accent's sounds (see the voices section in story-and-script) and repeat it in AUDIO and POSITIVE LOCKS |
| An entrance jumps between front and back views | Fix one camera per segment, state the direction of travel, keep the camera side rule |
| Long pauses slow the scene | Add "speech rides on top of action, no dead air" |
| A key reaction is lost (face turned away) | Move the camera closer and frame the listener's face; state that their face stays toward the camera |
| The face drifts | Use one face reference (erased front-view face), shorter segments, and attach the sheet every time |
| The clip has music | "No music anywhere in this clip, at any second; score added in the edit" |

Then generate the new take and **compare both versions side by side** before choosing.

## Edit
- Trim 0.5 s from the head and tail of each clip (warping lives there).
- Hard cuts for drama and comedy; match cuts for repeats; a cut to black for the cliffhanger.
- Freeze frames, crash zooms and punch-ins are edit effects.
- Add text, labels, subtitles (SRT), logos and the CTA as graphics inside the safe areas.
- Score and the sound-effect cue sheet: few effects, placed precisely; silence before a punchline.
- One grade per section and one grain pass over everything.
- Export: 1080x1920 for vertical, 1440x1080 for 4:3 drama, 1920x1080 for 16:9.

## Series and monetisation
- Episodes end on a cliffhanger, and free episodes create the demand for the next ones.
- Three income routes: making short dramas for clients, paid brand integrations (the product lives inside the story), and selling access to the next episodes (a monthly subscription, test 5 to 10 USD).
